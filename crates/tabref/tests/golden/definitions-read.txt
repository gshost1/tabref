# Meaning of gleam in English
gleam noun
(1/2) Save A gleam of light is a small, bright light that shines briefly: · the gleam of a lighthouse across the bay · a faint gleam in the dark
Synonyms glint flash
(2/2) Save a brief sign of a quality or emotion, seen in someone's eyes: · There was a hint of mischief in her eyes.
